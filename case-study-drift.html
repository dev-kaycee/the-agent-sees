Drift Coffee

A headless e-commerce experience that tripled online revenue for a specialty coffee roaster.

Client Drift Coffee
Industry Food & Beverage
Services Website, E-Commerce, UI/UX
Timeline 8 weeks

The Challenge

Drift Coffee's Shopify store was generic and slow. Their brand story wasn't coming through, mobile conversion was under 1%, and they had no subscription management.

The Solution

We designed and built a headless e-commerce site using Next.js and the Shopify Storefront API. Custom subscription logic, a coffee quiz for personalized recommendations, and a bean-origin story experience with scroll-driven animations. Automated order processing and inventory sync via n8n workflows connecting Shopify, their roastery software, and shipping providers.

The Results

3.2x Increase in online revenue
4.7% Mobile conversion rate (up from 0.9%)
60% Customers chose subscription
"Our website finally feels like our brand. And the automation saves us 20 hours a week."
Nina Torres Founder, Drift Coffee
