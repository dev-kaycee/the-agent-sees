Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 April 2026

1. Who We Are

The Agent Sees ("we", "us", "our") is a digital agency that designs and builds websites, web applications, and automated workflows for businesses. We are a remote-first company operating worldwide.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and what rights you have in relation to it. It applies to all visitors of our website at theagentsees.com and anyone who contacts us through the site.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us directly

When you fill out our contact form, we collect the following:

  • Full name
  • Company name (optional)
  • Email address
  • The service you are interested in
  • Any project details you include in your message

We only collect this information when you voluntarily submit the form. We do not collect personal information through any other means on this website.

Information collected automatically

When you visit our site, we use Microsoft Clarity, a behavioural analytics tool, to help us understand how visitors use the site. Clarity may collect the following data automatically:

  • Pages you visit and how long you spend on each
  • Clicks, taps, scrolls, and mouse movements
  • Device type, operating system, browser type, and screen resolution
  • Approximate geographic location (country or region level, not your precise location)
  • Referring URL (the page that brought you to our site)
  • Session recordings of how you interact with the page (anonymised)

This data is collected in aggregate and anonymised form. It does not identify you personally.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry — when you submit the contact form, we use your name, email, and message to get back to you and discuss your project.
  • To improve our website — analytics data helps us understand which pages and content are most useful, identify usability issues, and make the site better for future visitors.
  • To send follow-up communication — if you contact us about a project, we may follow up by email to continue the conversation. We will not add you to any marketing list without your explicit consent.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information on the following legal grounds:

  • Consent — by submitting the contact form, you consent to us processing your data to respond to your enquiry.
  • Legitimate interest — we have a legitimate interest in understanding how visitors use our website so we can improve it. This processing is minimal, anonymised, and does not override your rights.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. We share data only with the following third-party services that are necessary to operate the site:

  • FormSubmit.co — processes and delivers contact form submissions to our email inbox. Your form data passes through their servers during delivery. FormSubmit does not store your data permanently.
  • Microsoft Clarity — collects anonymised behavioural analytics data. Microsoft may process this data on servers outside your country of residence. For more information, see Microsoft's Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts — our site loads fonts from Google's servers. Google may collect your IP address and browser information when fonts are loaded. See Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not share your personal information with any other third parties unless required to do so by law.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our site uses cookies set by Microsoft Clarity to collect analytics data. These cookies help us understand visitor behaviour and are not used to identify you personally.

We do not use advertising cookies or social media tracking pixels.

Managing cookies: You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies entirely, delete existing cookies, or be notified before a cookie is set. Please note that disabling cookies may not affect the core functionality of our website.

7. Data Retention

  • Contact form submissions — your enquiry is delivered to our email inbox and retained for as long as needed to fulfil its purpose (typically the duration of our conversation about your project). We do not store form submissions in a separate database.
  • Analytics data — retained by Microsoft Clarity according to their standard data retention policies (typically up to 30 days for session recordings).

We will delete your personal information upon request, subject to any legal obligation to retain it.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration. Our website is served over HTTPS, which encrypts data transmitted between your browser and our server. Contact form submissions are transmitted securely to our email.

However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of access — you can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to correction — you can ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to deletion — you can ask us to delete your personal data. We will comply unless we have a legal reason to keep it.
  • Right to withdraw consent — you can withdraw your consent at any time. This will not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Right to object — you can object to our processing of your data based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to data portability — where applicable, you can request your data in a structured, commonly used format.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at info@theagentsees.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through our site, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. International Data Transfers

As a remote-first company, and through the third-party services we use, your data may be processed in countries other than your own. We ensure that any such transfers are handled in compliance with applicable data protection laws and that appropriate safeguards are in place.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:

Email: info@theagentsees.com

We will do our best to address your concern promptly and fairly.
