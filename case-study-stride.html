Stride Athletics

A member portal and class booking system that modernized a fitness brand's digital presence.

Client Stride Athletics
Industry Fitness & Wellness
Services Web App, API Integration, Automation
Timeline 10 weeks

The Challenge

Stride Athletics ran 50+ weekly classes across 3 locations but managed bookings via phone and email. No-shows were at 25%, and they had no way to track member engagement or automate communications.

The Solution

We built a custom member portal with React and a Node.js backend. Features include real-time class booking with waitlists, member profiles with workout history, automated reminder emails and SMS via Twilio integration, a trainer dashboard for class management, and Stripe integration for payments and membership management. Automated workflows handle no-show tracking, re-engagement campaigns, and monthly reporting.

The Results

6% No-show rate (down from 25%)
45% Increase in class bookings
92% Member satisfaction score
"The portal changed how our members interact with us. Bookings are up, no-shows are down, and we finally have data."
Rachel Kim Director, Stride Athletics
