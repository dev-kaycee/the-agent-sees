Meridian Finance

A client-facing fintech dashboard with automated compliance reporting and AI-powered insights.

Client Meridian Finance
Industry Financial Services
Services Web App, Automation, AI Agents, Security
Timeline 16 weeks

The Challenge

Meridian Finance managed portfolios for 2,000+ clients but their reporting was manual, compliance checks were error-prone, and clients had no self-service access to their portfolio data.

The Solution

We built a secure, SOC 2-compliant web application using React, Node.js, and PostgreSQL. Features include real-time portfolio visualization, automated compliance reporting via scheduled workflows, AI-powered market insight summaries generated by our intelligent agents, two-factor authentication, and audit logging. We also built an internal admin dashboard for advisors with automated client risk scoring and rebalancing alerts.

The Results

95% Client adoption in first month
15min Compliance reporting (was 2 days)
4.8/5 Client satisfaction score
"The Agent Sees didn't just build us a website. They transformed our entire digital workflow and tripled our conversion rate."
Sarah Chen CTO, Meridian Finance
