Luminate

A complete workflow automation system that eliminated 40 hours of manual work per week.

Client Luminate
Industry Creative Agency
Services Workflow Automation, Custom Integrations, AI Agents
Timeline 6 weeks

The Challenge

Luminate is a creative agency that was spending more time on admin than creative work. Client onboarding took 3 hours per client. Invoicing was manual. Project status updates were copy-pasted between 5 different tools.

The Solution

We built a comprehensive automation system using our proprietary orchestration layer. Client onboarding goes from form to fully set up in seconds: automated workspace creation in Notion, Slack channel setup, contract generation via PandaDoc, and welcome email sequences. For project updates, an AI-powered agent reads project data from Asana, Figma, and GitHub, then generates weekly client reports automatically. Invoice automation pulls time tracking data from Toggl through our automation engine to Xero, generating and sending invoices with zero manual input. Smart lead routing scores new inquiries with an AI agent and routes them to the right team member.

The Results

40hrs Saved per week
30sec Client onboarding (was 3 hours)
0 Invoicing errors since launch
"They automated our entire client onboarding. What used to take 3 hours now happens in 30 seconds."
Marcus Webb Operations Lead, Luminate
